# SPECDUMS Limited Governance & Financial Ledger Portal

Modular web portal for **SPECDUMS Limited** featuring:
1. **Executive Overview** (aggregate totals, quick jump cards, privacy status)
2. **Individual Member Ledger** (18-member baseline as of 11 Sep 2026, privacy masking + PIN unlock)
3. **3-Month Arrears Email Engine** (eligible members queue & reminder email generator)
4. **Cash Flow Forecasting** (multi-month cash projection model)
5. **Minutes & Resolutions** (record meeting minutes, resolutions passed, and voting results)
6. **Receipts & Projects** (log expense receipts and track project budgets/progress)
7. **Charity Contributions** (record charitable donations and community support)
